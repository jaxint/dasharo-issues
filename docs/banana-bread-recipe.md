# Banana Bread Recipe

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
2. Mash bananas in a large bowl
3. Mix in melted butter
4. Add sugar, egg, and vanilla - mix well
5. Stir in baking soda and salt
6. Add flour and mix until just combined
7. Pour into a greased 4x8 inch loaf pan
8. Bake for 50-60 minutes until golden brown
9. Cool before slicing

## Tips

- Use overripe bananas for best flavor
- Add walnuts or chocolate chips for variety

Enjoy! 🍌🍞
